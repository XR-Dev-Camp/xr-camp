## Asset policy

Every third-party asset — image, model, font, audio file, texture, code snippet — must be recorded in the relevant project's `ATTRIBUTION.md` before it is merged, with source, author, license, and any conditions.

Assets whose provenance cannot be established are not admitted, regardless of how freely available they appear. This applies equally to AI-generated assets: record the tool, the prompt, and the terms under which the output may be used.

Recording provenance is itself part of the curriculum. Learners are asked to do this in their own projects, so the repository must model it.
