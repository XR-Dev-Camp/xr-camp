# Commercial and Institutional Use

**Status: draft pending legal review.** This document describes intent. It is not a license and does not grant rights. The operative terms are in [`LICENSE-CONTENT`](../LICENSE-CONTENT) and [`LICENSE-CODE`](../LICENSE-CODE), and `LICENSE-CODE` is not yet finalised.

---

## Always permitted

You do **not** need permission to:

- Learn from this material, alone or in a study group.
- Complete the projects and publish your own versions.
- **Show your coursework in a portfolio, including one shown to prospective employers or clients.** Your coursework is yours.
- Use what you learned here in paid work. Skills are not licensed.
- Teach from this material without charge — a library, community group, school club, or refugee education programme.
- Translate the material and share the translation under the same terms.
- Fork this repository and adapt it for a non-commercial programme, with attribution.

## Requires a written agreement

Contact **partnerships@xrcamp.dev** if you intend to:

- Charge tuition for a course built on XR Camp curriculum.
- Include this material in a paid bootcamp, subscription, or training product.
- Offer accredited instruction using this curriculum.
- Deliver corporate training based on these materials.
- Resell, sublicense, or repackage the curriculum.
- Use XR Camp branding, certificates, or badge marks.

> **Setup required:** this address must exist and be monitored before the repository is made public.

## Institutional partnerships

Universities, schools, libraries, and nonprofits are welcomed. The intent is to make partnership straightforward, not to extract fees from educators. Where a partner cannot pay, we would rather grant use than see the material go unused — ask.

## The open question

The boundary between "learning from this" and "commercialising this" is genuinely hard to draw, and every noncommercial license in existence has struggled with it. Our bias is toward the learner. If your use is ambiguous and educational in spirit, ask and expect a yes.

## Certificates and credentials

XR Camp certificates are issued only by XR Camp through xrcamp.dev. No third party may issue XR Camp credentials, badges, or certificates of completion, under any license.
