# Women to Know: Editorial Roster

This is the editorial roster for the **Women to Know** spotlight in each XR Camp lesson. XR Camp's learners are mostly women in Central America, South America and China, so the roster leans on purpose toward Latin American and Chinese women. It also includes people from elsewhere in the Global South and a few whose work is essential to a particular topic.

**Status: fact-checked 25 September 2026.** Every entry was checked against at least two sources that were opened and read, not search snippets. The **Check** column shows the result: *Confirmed* (the original entry was accurate) or *Corrected* (the entry has been fixed; see **Verification notes** at the end). One candidate was removed; see **Notes / sensitivities**.

This is still an editorial roster, not publishable copy. Before any bio goes into a lesson, the editor must:

1. Re-check current job titles and employers, which change often. Several people changed roles in 2025–2026.
2. Where practical, contact the subject (or, for historical figures, their family or institution) to confirm the bio and how they want to be named, described and referred to (including pronouns).
3. Use the person's own spelling of their name. Give the native-script name where it applies, and do not "correct" diacritics.
4. Read the **Notes / sensitivities** section before placing anyone in a lesson.

Legend: **Era**: *pioneer* = historical or foundational figure; *active* = currently working in the field (as of 2026 sources).

Paths in the "Suggested lesson(s)" column are the course folder paths. Capstone folders are listed as `capstone/01–05` because their slugs were not given.
