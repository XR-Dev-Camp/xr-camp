# Where to share your work and ask for help

XR Camp does not have its own community space yet. It will grow as learners ask for one: if you would like an XR Camp group, say so in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/XR-Dev-Camp/xr-camp/issues), and tell us where you would use it.

Until then, these established communities are free, friendly to beginners, and full of people who build for the web. Read each one's code of conduct before you post.

## Ask questions and share projects

| Community | Good for | Languages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MDN Web Docs community](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/community) | Questions about HTML, CSS, JavaScript and web APIs, from the people who write the documentation you use in every lesson | English |
| [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/), [Stack Overflow en español](https://es.stackoverflow.com/), [Stack Overflow em português](https://pt.stackoverflow.com/) | Specific coding questions with a clear, small example. Search first: your question has often been answered | English, Spanish, Portuguese |
| [freeCodeCamp forum](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/) | Beginner questions, study buddies, and feedback on projects | Mostly English |
| [DEV Community](https://dev.to/) | Writing about what you built: a short post about a lesson project is a great portfolio piece | Many |
| [three.js forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/) | Questions about three.js (Phases 3 to 5) | English |
| [A-Frame community](https://aframe.io/community/) | Questions about A-Frame and WebXR scenes | English |
| [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/) | Local web, JavaScript, and XR groups that meet in person or online | Many |

## Staying safe online

- Share your code and screenshots, never passwords, keys, or your home address.
- You do not have to use your full name. A username is fine.
- If someone is unkind, block and report them. You never have to reply.

Know a community that should be here, especially one for women in Latin America or China? Suggest it in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/XR-Dev-Camp/xr-camp/issues).
