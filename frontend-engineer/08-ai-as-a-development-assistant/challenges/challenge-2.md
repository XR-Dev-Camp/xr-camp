# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Run a review in your own language, and build a glossary for your community.

## Task

1. Choose Spanish, Chinese, Portuguese, or the language you think in. Ask two of your review questions again, in that language, from the start.
2. Try an assistant from your region too, if you can: for example, one of the Chinese assistants from Step 2, or one your classmates use.
3. Collect ten technical words the assistant used, such as *callback*, *listener*, *live region*, or *pure function*. For each, write:
   - the word the assistant used, and the English word
   - the word MDN uses in your language, if that page is translated
   - one sentence of your own that explains it.
4. Mark each word in your page with its language, for example `<span lang="es">función pura</span>`, so screen readers pronounce it correctly.
5. Write three sentences: was the assistant as correct in your language? Were its words the ones your community uses?

## Why this matters

Learning in your own language is faster, but only if the words are right. A checked glossary helps the next learner too, and it is evidence of how well assistants serve languages other than English.

## Done when

- [ ] Two questions asked in your language, and logged.
- [ ] A glossary of ten words, each checked against MDN or another primary source.
- [ ] Every word in another language is marked with `lang`.
- [ ] Three sentences comparing the answers.
