# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Do one more small review, from start to finish, on your own.

## Task

1. Choose one file you have not reviewed yet: `planner/js/components/session-item.js`, `planner/js/utils.js`, or a small file from your Course 2.2 dashboard.
2. Before you open an assistant, explain the file in your own words, in three to five sentences.
3. Ask the assistant one question for an **explanation**, and one for a **review**. Keep each prompt small.
4. Write at least three review items in `review.md`, with all six parts. At least one must be **rejected**, with a link or a test that shows why. If every answer was right, ask a harder question: "What would break if…?"
5. Log both conversations in `ai-log.md`, and add the items to your review page.

## Why this matters

The first review follows the walkthrough. The second one is where it becomes your habit: read first, ask small, check everything, and write down the evidence.

## Done when

- [ ] Your own explanation was written before you asked.
- [ ] Three new review items, each with a source.
- [ ] At least one rejection, with evidence.
- [ ] Both conversations are in `ai-log.md`, and the items are on your page.
