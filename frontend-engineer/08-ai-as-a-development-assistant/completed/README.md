# Completed — AI as a Development Assistant

Ana's AI-assisted review. Ana is a fictional learner; the assistant answers
in her review are examples written by XR Camp. Open this after you have
done your own review.

- `review.md`: seven review items (two accepted, two changed, three
  rejected), a comparison in Spanish, and what she learned.
- `ai-log.md`: her rules, her assistant, and every use.
- `invented-apis.md`: the practice exercise, with each claim checked
  against MDN.
- `index.html`: the review as an accessible web page.
- `planner/`: the planner after her two changes (search for `Review item`).
- `3d-moment.html`: the three.js scene, with every claim in the AI
  explanation checked against the three.js docs.

Open everything through a local server (http://).
