A 3D viewer in your own element

Everything below the heading "The viewer" comes from one tag: <model-stage>. Its shape and colour are attributes. Its description is a paragraph you write inside it, so it is there even before the 3D scene loads, and even if it never does.

The scene already appears. Finish TODOs 13 and 14 in js/model-stage.js to connect its description, and to make its buttons work. The completed version is in completed/3d-moment.html.

The viewer

A purple ring, like a thick bracelet, floats at eye level above a pale floor, tilted a little towards you. It turns slowly around its centre, unless you pause it or have asked your device to reduce motion. Use Turn left and Turn right to turn it one step at a time.

How it is written

<model-stage shape="torus" color="#5b2a86" label="3D view: a purple ring">
  <p slot="description" id="scene-description">A purple ring …</p>
</model-stage>

Try changing shape to box, sphere, cone, cylinder, or dodecahedron in your browser's developer tools. The model changes at once. Then change the description to match: the words and the scene must never disagree.
