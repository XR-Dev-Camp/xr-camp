Everything in the exhibit

This list is not only a fallback: it is the same information as the picture, in words, so it is always here (WCAG 1.3.1).

    1. Look around

    Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the exhibit (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

    2. Animation

    The jade stone turns slowly on its own. Nothing else in the exhibit moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it.
