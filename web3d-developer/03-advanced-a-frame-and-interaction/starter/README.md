# Starter — Advanced A-Frame and Interaction

Begin here.

- `index.html` carries over the working room from [3.2](../../02-a-frame-foundations/completed/index.html): the sky, lights, floor, woven-pattern pedestal, English welcome panel, and the bell that will become the chime exhibit. The info panel, the "Select" buttons list, and the idle-animation and input-mode controls are already in the markup; they do nothing yet.
- `main.js` has 18 numbered TODOs, in the order the [README's walkthrough](../README.md) teaches them: TODO 1 (the exhibit data) through TODO 18 (the pause button). Each placeholder function is written so the page loads with no console errors before you start; it just does nothing until you replace it.
- TODOs 4, 10, and 15 are in `index.html`, not `main.js`: adding attributes to existing entities, a new exhibit, and the VR controller entities.
- Expected before you finish: nothing in the room responds to a click, a tap, or a keyboard "Select" button, the info panel never updates, and no sound plays. That is correct until the matching TODOs are done.

Open everything through a local server (http://).

Full instructions: [`../README.md`](../README.md).
