# Completed — Advanced A-Frame and Interaction

The reference solution. Open it after you have tried the starter.

- `index.html` and `main.js` are both finished: three interactive exhibits (the woven-pattern pedestal, a new story lantern, and a chime bell with positional audio), the `interactive-exhibit` custom component, the shared `selectExhibit()` function, a mouse/gaze cursor toggle, and `laser-controls` for VR.
- `styles.css` is [3.2](../../02-a-frame-foundations/completed/styles.css)'s stylesheet plus a labelled section for this lesson's info panel and controls.
- Try every input in turn: click or tap an exhibit, press its "Select" button, turn on the gaze cursor and hold your view on one, and (with the [Immersive Web Emulator](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/immersive-web-emulator/cgffilbpcibhmcfbgggfhfolhkfbhmik)) point a virtual controller's laser at one and pull its trigger. All four call the same `selectExhibit()`.

Open everything through a local server (http://).
