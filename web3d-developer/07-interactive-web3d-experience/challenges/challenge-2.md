# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Make the exhibit reflect your own culture, community, or language.

## Task

1. Replace at least one of the three primitive exhibits (clay pot, woven basket ring, or jade stone) with an object of your own choosing, from your own culture or community. Keep it a primitive built from three.js geometry (as the originals are), so no new asset or licence is needed; or, if you use a CC0/CC-BY glTF model instead, follow 3.5's rule exactly: check its licence file, stay within the 5 MB budget, and add it to `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the attribution page.
2. Update its entry in `js/exhibit.js` (name, material, and note) and its sentence in `js/describe.js` if you changed how many exhibits there are.
3. Write its info-panel text yourself: what it is, what it is made of, and why it matters to you or your community.
4. Update `CHANGELOG.md` with a short note describing your change.

## Why this matters

A virtual exhibit is more meaningful when it holds something the learner chose, not only the sample content everyone starts from. This is also good practice for the kind of small, well-documented change a real team makes to a shared project.

## Done when

- [ ] At least one exhibit is your own choice, with an accurate info panel.
- [ ] `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the attribution page are correct for anything new.
- [ ] `CHANGELOG.md` mentions the change.
- [ ] The page still passes every item in [`../tests/checklist.md`](../tests/checklist.md).
