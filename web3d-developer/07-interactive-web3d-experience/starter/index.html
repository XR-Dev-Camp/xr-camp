The virtual cultural exhibit

Five exhibits, built across 3.1 through 3.5, now on one polished page: a clay pot, a woven basket ring, and a jade stone built from primitives, joined by a fox figure and a Cesium milk truck, two real CC-licensed glTF models. Click or tap an exhibit to open its info panel, or use the Select buttons below. Each model plays its own built-in animation once it finishes loading.

The 2D twin

This list is not only a fallback: it is the same information as the picture, in words, so it is always here, whether or not WebGL works (WCAG 1.3.1).

    1. Look around

    Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the exhibit (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

    2. Select an exhibit

    Click or tap an exhibit in the 3D view, or choose it here. This is the keyboard route into the exhibit: it does exactly the same thing, without a pointer.

      4. Animation

      The jade stone turns on its own, and each model plays its own built-in animation once it loads. Nothing else moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it.

      5. Performance budget

      This capstone's budget, from the README: at most 20 draw calls and 25,000 triangles once every exhibit has loaded. Measured on the machine this lesson was written on, with a margin — see "Performance considerations" in the README.
