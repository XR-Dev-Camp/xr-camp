Everything in the hall

This list is not only a fallback: it is the same information as the picture, in words, so it is always here (WCAG 1.3.1).

    1. Look around

    Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the hall (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

    2. Travel

    Jump straight to a showcase or a wing. Each one is far enough from the main grid that walking there is the point.

      3. Animation

      The four showcase pieces turn slowly on their own. Nothing else in the hall moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it.

      4. Memory and performance

      Rebuilds the hall from scratch. Watch the Geometries and Textures counts below: in this starter, they climb every time and never come back down.
