Everything in the hall

This list is not only a fallback: it is the same information as the picture, in words, so it is always here (WCAG 1.3.1).

    1. Look around

    Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the hall (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

    2. Travel

    Jump straight to a showcase or a wing. Walking out to a wing is what loads it; walking back away from it is what unloads it again.

      3. Animation

      The four showcase pieces turn slowly on their own. Nothing else in the hall moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it or ask it to travel.

      4. Memory and performance

      Disposes the main grid and builds it again. Watch "Geometries" and "Textures" below: they return to the same numbers, which is how you prove nothing leaked.
