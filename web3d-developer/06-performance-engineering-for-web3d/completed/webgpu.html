Bonus: WebGPU and TSL

This optional extra swaps the hall's WebGLRenderer for WebGPURenderer (imported from three/webgpu) and colours one cube with a small shader written in TSL (Three.js Shading Language, imported from three/tsl) instead of a plain material. Read "Going further: WebGPU" in the lesson README for the full explanation. This page is not part of the required lesson.
