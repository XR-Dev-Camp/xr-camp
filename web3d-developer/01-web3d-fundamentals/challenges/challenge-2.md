# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 60 minutes.

An object from your own culture, built from primitive shapes.

## Task

1. Choose an object that matters where you are from: a molcajete, a clay jar, a drum, a lantern, a teapot, a woven basket, a pot for maté.
2. Build it in the lab, or in a new page, using only primitives (boxes, cylinders, spheres, cones, tori), grouped under one parent `Group`, so it moves as one.
3. Use real sizes, in metres. Look up or measure the real object.
4. Choose materials carefully: is it matte clay (Lambert, or Standard with high roughness), polished metal (Standard, high metalness, low roughness), or painted wood?
5. Write its scene description, as you would for a museum label: what it is, what it is made of, its size, and why it matters to you. Include its name in your language.

## Why this matters

This object may become part of your virtual exhibit. Building from primitives teaches you to see any object as simple shapes, which is how 3D artists start before they model details.

## Done when

- [ ] Your object, built from grouped primitives, at real size.
- [ ] Materials chosen for what it is made of.
- [ ] A museum-style description, with its name in your language.
