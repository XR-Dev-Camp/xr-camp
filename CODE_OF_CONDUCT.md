# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

XR Camp exists to help people learn — including people who have never written a line of code, people learning in their second or third language, and people who have been made to feel unwelcome in technical spaces before.

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the person asking has good reason to ask.
- Answer beginner questions generously. Everyone here was a beginner recently.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept correction gracefully, including in public.
- Respect that many participants are writing in a non-native language.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks; sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing or belittling someone for what they do not yet know.
- Presenting AI-generated work as your own understanding when asked directly.

## In immersive spaces

XR Camp community events may take place in shared 3D or XR environments. There, additionally: respect personal space and boundaries, do not follow or crowd other participants, do not use spatial audio to harass, and honour any comfort or safety settings another participant has enabled.

## Reporting

Report violations to **conduct@xrcamp.dev**.

> **Setup required:** this address must exist and be monitored before the repository is made public. Reports must reach a named, accountable person.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will receive acknowledgement within three working days. You may report conduct by maintainers, instructors, or organisers — including conduct by the people who wrote this document.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will follow these steps in proportion to the impact of the behaviour:

1. **Correction** — a private written warning explaining the violation.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with those involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — from all community spaces and contribution channels.
4. **Permanent ban** — from all public participation in XR Camp.

## Scope

This applies in all XR Camp spaces — this repository, the website at xrcamp.dev, community channels, immersive environments, and public events — and when an individual is representing XR Camp in public.
