My path into the spatial web

The roles that interest me, the skills they need, and my plan to get there. By Ana.

The roles I am aiming for

  1. Web3D and XR developer, because I want to build the museum of my town's history that nobody has built yet.
  2. Accessibility specialist, because my grandmother is blind and most websites shut her out.
  3. Developer advocate and educator, because one day I want to teach at XR Camp.

My pathway

  1. By March: finish Phase 0, and publish this page.
  2. By November: finish Phase 1, with a portfolio of accessible websites.
  3. Next year: Phases 2 and 3, and my first 3D exhibit about my town.
  4. The year after: Phase 4, and my exhibit in VR.

People to learn from

Roles in the spatial web

The twelve roles I explored. The ones I am aiming for have a thick border.

  • Frontend developer

    Builds the parts of websites and apps that people see and use.

    XR Camp: Phases 1–2

  • Web and UX designer

    Designs how products look, feel, and work, based on research with real users.

    XR Camp: every phase, especially 1 and 4

  • Accessibility specialist

    Makes sure products work for disabled people, and tests with assistive technology.

    XR Camp: every lesson, especially 1.5 and 4.4

  • JavaScript engineer

    Writes the logic behind interactive applications.

    XR Camp: Phases 1, 2, and 5

  • Web3D and XR developer

    Builds 3D and immersive experiences that run in a browser.

    XR Camp: Phases 3 and 4

  • Spatial UX designer

    Designs how people move, point, and feel comfortable in 3D and XR.

    XR Camp: Course 4.3

  • Technical artist

    Connects art and code: prepares 3D models, materials, and effects so they look good and run fast.

    XR Camp: Phase 3

  • Geospatial developer

    Builds maps and applications that work with location data.

    XR Camp: the optional geospatial track

  • Digital twin developer

    Builds live 3D copies of real buildings, factories, or cities, connected to sensor data.

    XR Camp: Phases 3 and 5

  • AI-assisted developer

    Uses AI tools to build faster, while checking and understanding every result.

    XR Camp: Courses 2.8 and 5.6

  • Standards contributor

    Helps write the web's standards, in organisations like W3C.

    XR Camp: Courses 0.7 and 6.3

  • Developer advocate and educator

    Teaches developers, writes documentation, and builds communities.

    XR Camp: Courses 6.3 and 6.5
