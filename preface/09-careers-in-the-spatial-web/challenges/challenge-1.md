# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Interview someone who works in technology.

## Task

1. Find someone who works with technology: a web developer, a designer, an IT technician, a teacher who uses technology. Ask a friend, a family member, or someone at a library or community centre.
2. Ask them, in person, by message, or by video call:
   - What does a normal day look like?
   - How did you start?
   - What do you wish you had learned sooner?
3. Write their answers in your journal. Ask before you share their name.

## Why this matters

Job titles hide what people actually do. Ten minutes with someone who does the work tells you more than any list, and it is how professional networks begin.

## Done when

- [ ] Your journal has answers to all three questions.
- [ ] You thanked them.
