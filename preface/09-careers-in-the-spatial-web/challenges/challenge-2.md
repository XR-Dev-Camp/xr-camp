# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Map the technology roles in your own region.

## Task

1. Look at job listings, local companies, museums, universities, and nonprofits in your city or country.
2. Which of the twelve roles can you find? Which roles exist there that are not in the list?
3. Add a section to your career page called "In my region", with what you found.

## Why this matters

Opportunities look different in Lima, São Paulo, Guadalajara, and Chengdu. A plan based on your own region, and on remote work beyond it, is more realistic than one based on someone else's.

## Done when

- [ ] Your page has a section about roles in your region.
- [ ] It names at least three real organisations or listings.
