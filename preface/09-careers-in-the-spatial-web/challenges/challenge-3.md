# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Turn your roles into a 3D constellation.

## Task

1. Start from your `my-first-world` page from Course 0.1.
2. Replace the shapes with twelve small spheres, one per role, spread out like stars. Make your three chosen roles bigger and brighter.
3. Give the sky a night colour, such as `#1b1b3a`.
4. Rewrite the scene description: which star is which, and which ones are yours.

## Why this matters

You are practising the idea of Course 0.6: the same information shown in 2D (your career page) and in 3D (your constellation). Ask yourself honestly which one explains your plan better.

## Done when

- [ ] Twelve spheres, three of them bigger and brighter.
- [ ] A scene description that names your three roles.
