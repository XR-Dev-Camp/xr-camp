# Completed — Welcome to XR Camp

The reference solution. Open it **after** you have built your own world.

## What this is

Ana's first 3D world: a pink evening sky, a purple box, a golden cylinder, a
teal cone, and a white moon that rises and sinks gently.

Compare it with the starter and you will see every TODO done: a new title and
heading, a new sky, moved and recoloured shapes, an added cone, and a
description rewritten to match.

## Comparing against your own work

Your world will look nothing like Ana's, and that is the point. Check only:

- Does your description match what is actually in your world?
- Does the **Pause animation** button still stop the movement?
- Can you still move with the keyboard?

If yes, your version is correct.
