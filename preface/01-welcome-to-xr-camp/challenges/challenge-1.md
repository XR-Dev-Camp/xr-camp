# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 20 minutes.

Add two more shapes and describe them.

## Task

1. Add two new shapes to your world. A-Frame has many: `a-torus` (a ring), `a-dodecahedron`, `a-octahedron`, `a-torus-knot`. Try them.
2. Give each one its own colour and position.
3. Update your scene description so it mentions both.

## Why this matters

Every time the world changes, the description must change too. Making that a habit now, while your world has five shapes, is far easier than fixing it later when it has five hundred.

## Done when

- [ ] Your world has at least five shapes.
- [ ] No shape sits inside another.
- [ ] Your description mentions every shape.
