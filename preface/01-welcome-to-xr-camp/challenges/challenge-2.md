# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** As long as you like.

Build a place from your own life.

## Task

1. Choose a place that matters to you: your street, a market, a kitchen, a school, a park.
2. Rebuild it using only simple shapes. A table can be a flat box on four thin cylinders. A tree can be a cone on a cylinder.
3. Choose colours that feel like that place, and a sky for the time of day you remember it.
4. Write the description in the language you would use to describe that place to a friend. If it is not English, change `lang="en"` at the top of the file to your language: `es` for Spanish, `zh-Hans` for Simplified Chinese, `pt` for Portuguese.

## Why this matters

The spatial web should include your places and your language, not only someone else's. And `lang` tells a screen reader how to pronounce your words: Spanish read aloud by an English voice is very hard to understand.

## Done when

- [ ] Someone who knows the place can recognise it.
- [ ] The description is in your chosen language, and `lang` matches it.
