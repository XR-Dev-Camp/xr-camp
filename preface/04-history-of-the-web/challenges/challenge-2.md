# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Interview someone about their first time online.

## Task

1. Ask someone older than you: a parent, a grandparent, a neighbour, a teacher.
2. Ask: When was your first time online? Where were you? What did you do? What did it feel like? What has changed since?
3. Write their story as one or two milestones in "My own milestones", in their words where you can.
4. Ask their permission before you share their name. "My grandmother" is fine if they prefer.

## Why this matters

Most of the web's history has never been written down. It lives in people's memories. Asking is how it gets recorded, and asking permission is how it gets recorded respectfully.

## Done when

- [ ] At least one milestone tells someone else's story.
- [ ] You have their permission to share it, with or without their name.
