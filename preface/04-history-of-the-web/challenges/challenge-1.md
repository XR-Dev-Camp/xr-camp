# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Add your country's first internet connection to your timeline.

## Task

1. Find out when your country first connected to the internet. Search in your own language, for example "primera conexión a internet en México", "primeira conexão à internet no Brasil", or "中国第一次接入互联网".
2. Find two sources that agree. Prefer a university, a national archive, or the organisation that made the connection.
3. Add it to the right era of your timeline, with a correct `datetime`.
4. Write both sources in your learner journal.

## Why this matters

The history of the web is usually told from California and Switzerland. It happened in your country too, and someone made it happen. Finding that story, and checking it, is a skill you will use for the rest of your career.

## Done when

- [ ] The milestone is in the right era, in date order.
- [ ] Its `datetime` is written year-month-day, as far as you know the date.
- [ ] Two sources are noted in your journal.
