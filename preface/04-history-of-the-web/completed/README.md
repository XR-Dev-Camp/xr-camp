# Completed — History of the Web

The reference solution. Open it **after** you have built your own timeline.

## What this is

Ana's timeline: 30 milestones across five eras, plus two from her own life.
Every fact in it is explained, with sources, in the lesson guide.

## Comparing against your own work

Your timeline does not need the same milestones as Ana's. Check only:

- Is every milestone a complete `<li>` with a `<time datetime>`, a heading, and a sentence?
- Is each era in date order?
- Could someone check every fact you added, using the sources in your journal?

If yes, your version is correct.
