# Project check

Work through this list before you submit.

## Your timeline
- [ ] Your name is in the title and in the introduction.
- [ ] Every era has at least three milestones, and "My own milestones" has at least two.
- [ ] Every era's milestones are in date order.
- [ ] You have noted a source for every milestone you added yourself.

## Structure
- [ ] Every milestone is a complete `<li>` block inside the right `<ol>`.
- [ ] Every `<time>` has a `datetime` written year-month-day.
- [ ] Headings are in order: one `h1`, eras as `h2`, milestones as `h3`.
- [ ] `lang` at the top of the file matches the language of your text.

## Accessibility and layout
- [ ] Press Tab once: the "Skip to the timeline" link appears.
- [ ] You can reach every era link with the keyboard, and they jump to the right place.
- [ ] At phone width, nothing needs sideways scrolling.
