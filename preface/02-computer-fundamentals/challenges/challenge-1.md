# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 20 minutes.

A two-minute keyboard-only drill.

## Task

Put your mouse or trackpad out of reach. Using only the keyboard:

1. Open a new browser tab and go to this lesson.
2. Select a paragraph, copy it, and paste it into your text editor.
3. Save the file, switch back to the browser, and reload.
4. Press **Tab** until you reach the "Folder guide" link on the lesson page, and press **Enter**.
5. Take a screenshot.

Time yourself. Then do it again tomorrow and see how much faster you are.

## Why this matters

Speed is nice, but the real lesson is what it feels like to use a computer without a mouse, as many people do every day. The pages you build must work for them.

## Done when

- [ ] You completed all five steps without touching the mouse.
- [ ] You wrote both times in your learner journal.
