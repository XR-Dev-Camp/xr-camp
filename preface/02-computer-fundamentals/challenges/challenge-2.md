# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Your setup page and folders, in your own language.

## Task

1. Rewrite your setup page in the language you think in: Spanish, Chinese, Portuguese, or any other.
2. Change `lang="en"` at the top of the file to match: `es`, `zh-Hans`, `pt`.
3. Keep your **folder names** in plain lower-case letters without accents or spaces, even in your language: `mi-primer-mundo`, not `Mi Primer Mundo`. Explain on your page why.

## Why this matters

You will build for people who speak many languages. The words on a page can be in any language, but file names and code follow stricter rules, because computers and web servers read them. Knowing which is which is a real professional skill.

## Done when

- [ ] Your page is in your language, and `lang` matches.
- [ ] Your folder names still use only lower-case letters, numbers, and hyphens.
