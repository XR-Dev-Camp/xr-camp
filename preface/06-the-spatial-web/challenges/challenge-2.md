# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Design an immersive idea for your community, and decide honestly whether it should be immersive.

## Task

1. Think of a real need in your community: a local history nobody has recorded, a market that is hard to find, a skill people want to learn safely.
2. Describe an idea for each dimension: a 2D page, an interactive 3D experience, and an immersive one.
3. Choose the best one, and explain why. It is fine, and often right, to choose 2D.
4. Add your idea to your page as a new section.

## Why this matters

The best spatial developers are not the ones who use XR everywhere, but the ones who know exactly when it helps. That judgement starts now.

## Done when

- [ ] Your page has a section with your idea in all three dimensions.
- [ ] You chose one, and gave a reason.
