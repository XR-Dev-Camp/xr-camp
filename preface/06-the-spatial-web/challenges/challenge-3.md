# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Try your page in VR with a browser emulator.

## Task

1. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, install the free **Immersive Web Emulator** extension, published by Meta, from your browser's extension store.
2. Open your developer tools (**F12**, or **⌘ + Option + I** on a Mac). A new **WebXR** tab appears.
3. Reload your page. The **View in VR** button now appears, because the emulator pretends to be a headset.
4. Press it. Use the emulator's panel to move the pretend headset and controllers, and look at your object from inside.

## Why this matters

Most developers do not own every headset. Emulators let you test immersive pages on an ordinary computer. You will use one throughout Phase 4.

## Done when

- [ ] The VR button appeared with the emulator on.
- [ ] You took a screenshot from inside your immersive view.
