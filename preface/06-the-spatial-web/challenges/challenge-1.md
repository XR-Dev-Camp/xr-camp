# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Classify five real products.

## Task

1. Think of five apps, games, websites, or devices you have used or heard of: a map app, a video game, a filter that puts animal ears on your face, a museum website, a headset game.
2. For each one, decide: is it 2D, interactive 3D, VR, AR, or MR? Some are more than one.
3. For each one, write one sentence: would it be better, worse, or the same in a different dimension?

## Why this matters

The words VR, AR, and MR are used loosely in advertising. Being precise about them is part of being a professional, and part of making honest choices for the people you build for.

## Done when

- [ ] Five products are classified in your journal.
- [ ] Each one has a sentence about whether a different dimension would help.
