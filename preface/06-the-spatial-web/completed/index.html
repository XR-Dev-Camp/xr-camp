One object, three ways

A tomato plant, shown as a flat page, as interactive 3D, and as an immersive experience. By Ana.

1. The 2D view

Tomato plant

Grows on my balcony in Lima. Needs sun every day and water every two days. Ready to pick after about ten weeks.

Good for: reading quickly, searching, printing, and every device and assistive technology.

2. The interactive 3D view

The same tomato plant in 3D: a terracotta pot, a green stem, a round green bush, and three red tomatoes. Drag with a mouse or a finger to look around it. Nothing moves on its own.

Good for: understanding shape, size, and how parts fit together.

3. The immersive view

Checking whether this device can show virtual or augmented reality…

Good for: feeling real size and presence, and seeing an object in your own space.

When not to use 3D or XR

Opening times, a bus timetable, or a form do not need 3D. If the 3D does not help people understand something they could not understand from a picture and a sentence, it only makes the page slower and harder to use. Start in 2D, and add dimensions only when they help.
