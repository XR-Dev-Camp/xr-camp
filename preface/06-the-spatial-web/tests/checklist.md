# Project check

Work through this list before you submit.

## Your page
- [ ] Your name is in the title and the introduction.
- [ ] Your object is from your own life or region, in all three views.
- [ ] Every "?" in the comparison table is replaced.
- [ ] You gave two examples of things that should not be 3D or XR.

## Structure
- [ ] The SVG drawing has a `<title>` that describes your object.
- [ ] Headings are in order: one `h1`, sections as `h2`.
- [ ] `lang` at the top of the file matches your language.

## 3D and XR (manual)

Automated tools cannot see inside a 3D canvas, so these are checked by a person. See [`docs/en/xr-accessibility.md`](../../../docs/en/xr-accessibility.md).

- [ ] The scene description (`id="scene-description"`) matches your 3D object exactly.
- [ ] Nothing in the scene moves on its own.
- [ ] The page still makes sense without the 3D view: the 2D card and the table say everything important.
- [ ] The VR and AR buttons appear only on devices that support them.
- [ ] The camera never moves unless you move it.
