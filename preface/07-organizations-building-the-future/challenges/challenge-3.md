# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Join a W3C Community Group.

## Task

1. Create a free W3C account at [w3.org](https://www.w3.org/).
2. Browse the [Community Groups](https://www.w3.org/community/groups/) and choose one that interests you: the Immersive Web Community Group, or a group about accessibility or your language.
3. Join it, and read its most recent discussions.
4. In your journal, write one thing you understood and one thing you did not.

## Why this matters

This is where the future of the web is discussed, in public. You do not have to speak in your first week: reading is how everyone starts, and your questions are useful, because standards must make sense to beginners too.

## Done when

- [ ] You joined one Community Group.
- [ ] Your journal has one thing you understood and one you did not.
