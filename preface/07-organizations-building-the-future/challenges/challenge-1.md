# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Find three standards you used today without knowing it.

## Task

1. Think back over today: messages you sent, pages you read, photos you took, maps you opened.
2. Find three standards behind those things. For example: HTTPS when you opened a website, JPEG when you took a photo, UTF-8 when you typed in Spanish or Chinese.
3. For each one, find which organisation maintains it, and add it to your journal.

## Why this matters

Standards are invisible when they work. Noticing them is the first step to understanding why your pages work everywhere, and what happens when something does not.

## Done when

- [ ] Your journal lists three standards you used today.
- [ ] Each one names the organisation that maintains it.
