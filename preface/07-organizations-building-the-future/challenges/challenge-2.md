# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Add an organisation from your own region.

## Task

1. Find an organisation in your country or region that shapes the internet or the web: a national domain registry (such as NIC México, NIC.br, or CNNIC), a regional internet registry (LACNIC for Latin America and the Caribbean, APNIC for Asia-Pacific), a standards body, or a community of developers.
2. Add it to your map as a new card, with a sentence, its technologies or work, and how people can take part.
3. Give it a sensible `data-tags` value. Add a new filter button if you need one.

## Why this matters

The web is often described as if it were built only in a few countries. It is maintained all over the world, including in your region, and those organisations are where local needs are heard.

## Done when

- [ ] Your map has a card for an organisation from your region.
- [ ] It appears with at least one filter.
