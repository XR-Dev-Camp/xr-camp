# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Trace the undersea cables that bring the internet to your country.

## Task

1. Open the [Submarine Cable Map](https://www.submarinecablemap.com/), a free map of the cables under the oceans.
2. Find the cables that land in or near your country. Where do they come from?
3. Add a short section to your diagram called "The internet in my country", with what you found.

## Why this matters

"The cloud" is not in the sky: most international internet traffic travels through cables on the sea floor. Knowing that the internet is physical explains why some places have faster, cheaper access than others.

## Done when

- [ ] Your diagram has a section about your country's connection.
- [ ] It names at least one cable or landing point.
