# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Take apart five real web addresses.

## Task

1. Copy five addresses from your browser: a search result, a video, a news article, a map, and one of your own choice.
2. For each one, write down its scheme, domain, path, and query (if it has one).
3. Find one address whose domain ends in your country's code, such as `.mx`, `.br`, `.pe`, `.co`, `.ar`, or `.cn`.

## Why this matters

Reading an address carefully is how you notice a scam: `bank.example.com.scam.net` is not your bank. It is also how you will debug broken links for the rest of your career.

## Done when

- [ ] Five addresses are taken apart in your journal.
- [ ] One of them uses your country's domain.
