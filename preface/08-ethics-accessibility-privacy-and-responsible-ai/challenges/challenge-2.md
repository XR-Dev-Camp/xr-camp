# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Your pledge, in your own language, shared with someone.

## Task

1. Write your pledge in the language you think in. If it is not English, set `lang` on the pledge's `<section>`, for example `<section lang="es">`, so screen readers switch pronunciation for that part only.
2. Read it aloud to someone you trust: a friend, a family member, another learner.
3. Ask them: which promise matters most to you as a user? Add their answer to your journal.

## Why this matters

A pledge you have said aloud to someone is one you are more likely to keep. And setting `lang` on part of a page is a real technique for multilingual sites.

## Done when

- [ ] Your pledge is in your language, with the right `lang`.
- [ ] Your journal records someone else's answer.
