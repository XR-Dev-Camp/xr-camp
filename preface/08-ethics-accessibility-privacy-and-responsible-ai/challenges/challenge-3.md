# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Test an AI tool for bias in your language.

## Task

1. Choose an AI tool you can use for free: a chatbot, a translation tool, or an image generator.
2. Ask it the same question in English and in your own language, for example "Describe a software engineer" or "Draw a scientist".
3. Compare the answers. Do they differ? Who is shown, and who is missing?
4. Try three different questions. Record what you asked, and what you noticed, in your journal.

## Why this matters

AI systems usually work best in the languages and for the people most present in their training data. Testing in your own language is a small contribution to making them fairer, and a habit you will need as a developer.

## Done when

- [ ] Your journal has three questions asked in two languages.
- [ ] You wrote one sentence about what you noticed.
