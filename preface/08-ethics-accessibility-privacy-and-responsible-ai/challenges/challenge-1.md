# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Audit a real website you use.

## Task

1. Choose a real website you use often: a shop, a news site, a government service, a school portal.
2. Audit it the same way you audited MegaDeals: keyboard only, read everything, fill in (but do not submit) any form, wait and reload.
3. Add a new section to your report called "A real website", with what you found and how you would fix it.
4. Describe what you found, not the people who made it: they may not know, and many teams want to fix these problems.

## Why this matters

MegaDeals made the problems easy to find. Real sites hide them better, and many of the people you build for use those sites every day. Spotting problems in real products is the skill employers are looking for.

## Done when

- [ ] Your report has a section about a real website.
- [ ] It includes at least one accessibility, one privacy, and one design finding, each with a fix.
