# Stage 1 rubric: Capstone Research and Definition

| Level | Problem statement | Scope | Stakeholder map | Mentor approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Not yet** | Vague or missing; no clear audience | No "out of scope" list, or fewer than three items | Missing stakeholders who clearly affect the project | Not sent, or no decision recorded |
| **Meets expectations** | Names a specific audience and their problem | At least three items in scope and three out of scope, each with a reason | Every obvious stakeholder listed, with their stake and decisions | Sent, and a decision (approved, approved with changes, or not yet) is recorded |
| **Strong** | A mentor could repeat it back accurately after one read | Scope choices are clearly linked to the five-stage timeline | Includes a communication plan matched to each stakeholder's role | Approved, with any requested changes visibly made |
| **Exceptional** | Elevator pitch (Challenge 1) makes the "why now" obvious | Scope reflects real user input (Challenge 3) or personal context (Challenge 2) | Stakeholder map anticipates a conflict of interest and how it will be handled | Approval includes specific, substantive feedback that shaped the final brief |

This stage is approved when your reviewer can answer, from your documents alone: who is this for, what will you build, what will you not build, and what happens if it goes wrong.
