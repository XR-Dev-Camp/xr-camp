# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Make it personal: root your capstone in a place, language, or community you actually know.

## Task

1. Add a short section to `brief.md` called "Why this place, why me": one or two paragraphs connecting your capstone to a real place, language, or community you have a personal link to (your city, your family's town, your school, a community group you belong to).
2. Name at least one detail that would be different if this capstone were built by someone unfamiliar with that place — a local landmark, a language choice, a cultural practice, a specific accessibility need in your community.
3. Check that this detail is reflected somewhere concrete in your scope (Step 3 of the walkthrough), not only in this new paragraph.

## Why this matters

The strongest capstones are not generic: they carry something the author alone could bring. Naming your own context early makes your later design and prototype decisions sharper, and it gives you a genuine story to tell in Stage 5's case study.

## Done when

- [ ] The brief names a real place, language, or community you are personally connected to.
- [ ] At least one concrete design or scope decision reflects that connection.
- [ ] Nothing about a real person or organisation is stated as fact unless it is true.
