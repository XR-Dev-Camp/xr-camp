# Stakeholder map

| Stakeholder | Their stake | What they can decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Museum visitors (online) | Want to see the collection without travelling; some use screen readers or slow connections | Whether the exhibit is usable enough that they keep exploring it |
| Museum staff (fictional partner) | Want the collection represented accurately and respectfully | What objects are included, and how they are described |
| XR Camp mentor | Assesses each stage against the rubric before the next stage begins | Whether a stage is approved, approved with changes, or not yet approved |
| Ana (the learner) | Builds and maintains the exhibit across all five stages | All technical and design decisions within the approved scope |

## Communication plan

| Stakeholder | How | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Museum visitors (online) | Represented through the accessibility and audience notes above, and tested with real screen-reader and low-bandwidth checks | Every stage, as part of testing |
| Museum staff (fictional partner) | A short written summary at the end of each stage (in a real partnership, this would be an actual meeting or email) | Once per stage |
| XR Camp mentor | Brief and stakeholder map shared for review; approval recorded in `mentor-approval.md` | Once per stage, before moving on |
| Ana (the learner) | Own learner journal, updated each session | Every session |
