# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Test your prototype with the keyboard alone.

## Task

1. Unplug your mouse, or disable your trackpad, and try to complete every interaction your prototype offers using only the keyboard.
2. In `test-log.md`, log every place you got stuck: a control with no visible focus, a button that could not be reached by tabbing, an interaction with no keyboard equivalent at all.
3. Fix at least the most serious issue you found before moving on, and log the fix.

## Why this matters

A prototype that "mostly" works by keyboard is, for a keyboard-only or switch-device user, a prototype that does not work at all. Testing this yourself, honestly, catches problems long before Stage 4 makes them expensive to fix.

## Done when

- [ ] A full keyboard-only walkthrough was attempted and logged.
- [ ] At least one issue found was fixed, and the fix is logged.
- [ ] Every interaction in the prototype now has a working keyboard route.
