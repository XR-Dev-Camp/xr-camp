# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional stretch.** Roughly 90 minutes.

Add a second object type and re-measure your performance budget.

## Task

1. Add a second, visually different object to your prototype (a different primitive geometry and material), positioned so both are visible together.
2. Re-measure draw calls and triangles with `renderer.info`, and log the new numbers in `test-log.md` next to your original measurement.
3. If you are now over your Stage 2 budget, note what you would simplify in Stage 4 to bring it back under budget — you do not need to fix it now, only plan for it.

## Why this matters

A budget checked against one object tells you little about a scene with many. Testing with two is the smallest possible step toward knowing whether your budget will hold as Stage 4 adds the rest of your exhibit.

## Done when

- [ ] A second object is visible in the prototype alongside the first.
- [ ] Draw calls and triangles are measured and logged for both configurations.
- [ ] If over budget, a specific simplification plan is written for Stage 4.
