# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Prototype a detail that reflects your own language or community.

## Task

1. Add one label, description, or detail to your prototype's data that is specific to your own language, community, or culture (for example, a locally meaningful object name, a culturally specific description, or a label in a script other than plain ASCII — remember A-Frame's built-in font drops accents and Chinese, so use an HTML overlay or canvas text if you need this in a 3D label).
2. Show it to someone outside that community and note, in `design-review.md`, whether it made sense to them without extra explanation.
3. Adjust the description if it did not.

## Why this matters

A capstone that only makes sense to its author has not yet found its real audience. Testing a culturally specific detail against someone unfamiliar with it tells you whether your explanation, not just your reference, is doing its job.

## Done when

- [ ] The prototype includes at least one detail rooted in your own language or community.
- [ ] Someone outside that community reviewed it and their reaction is logged.
- [ ] Any non-ASCII text in a 3D label uses an HTML overlay or canvas text, not A-Frame's built-in text geometry.
