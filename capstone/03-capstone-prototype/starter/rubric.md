# Stage 3 rubric: Capstone Prototype

| Level | Prototype plan | Working prototype | Testing | Design review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Not yet** | No clear "riskiest thing" named | Renders, but console errors or no keyboard route exist | No test log, or only informal notes | Not held, or no record |
| **Meets expectations** | Names specific risks and what "proved" looks like | Renders cleanly; every interaction has a keyboard route; reduced motion respected | Keyboard, screen reader, and performance all logged with dates | Held and recorded, including feedback |
| **Strong** | Scope is clearly smaller than the full capstone, with explicit cuts | Scene description and 2D twin match the 3D view exactly | Localisation readiness explicitly checked | Specific changes made in response to feedback, and logged |
| **Exceptional** | Plan reflects a genuine, named risk from Stage 2 | Passes a full keyboard-only walkthrough (Challenge 1) | Performance re-measured with a second object (Challenge 3) | Feedback led to a documented design change, not just cosmetic notes |

This stage is approved when the prototype proves its named risks with evidence in the test log, and the design review shows the plan was genuinely open to changing.
