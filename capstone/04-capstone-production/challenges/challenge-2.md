# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Add a production detail rooted in your own language or community.

## Task

1. Add one label, description, or interaction detail to your production build that reflects your own language, community, or culture — something more specific than a generic placeholder.
2. Show it to someone outside that background and confirm, in your changelog, whether it read clearly without extra explanation.
3. Revise it if it did not, and note the revision.

## Why this matters

A finished capstone is a chance to bring something only you could build. Confirming it reads clearly to an outsider is what turns a personal detail into a genuinely inclusive one.

## Done when

- [ ] The production build includes at least one detail rooted in your own language or community.
- [ ] It was checked with someone outside that background.
- [ ] The changelog records the check and any revision made.
