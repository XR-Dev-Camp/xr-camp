# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional stretch.** Roughly 90 minutes.

Plan one more stage, even though you will not build it.

## Task

1. Write a short "If I had one more stage" section at the end of `retrospective.md`: the single most valuable thing you would build or improve next, and why you chose it over other options.
2. Sketch, in two or three sentences, what a Stage 2-style design document for that next step would need to cover.
3. Note what would need to change in your Stage 1 scope to make room for it.

## Why this matters

A finished capstone is rarely a finished product — most real projects keep evolving. Planning the next step, even briefly, is good practice for scoping future work realistically, and gives you something concrete to mention if anyone asks "what's next?"

## Done when

- [ ] One clear, prioritised next step is named, with a reason for choosing it.
- [ ] A brief sketch of what designing it would involve is written.
- [ ] The retrospective connects it back to the original Stage 1 scope.
