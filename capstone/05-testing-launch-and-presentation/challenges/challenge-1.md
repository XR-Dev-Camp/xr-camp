# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Present your capstone to someone who has never seen it.

## Task

1. Give your presentation (from `presentation-outline.md`) to a friend, family member, or classmate who has never seen your capstone, timed.
2. Do not explain anything beyond your prepared material.
3. Record, in `presentation-outline.md`, what questions they asked afterward, and whether your outline already answered them.

## Why this matters

A presentation that makes sense to you, who built the project, proves little. A first-time listener's questions tell you exactly what your outline still assumes the audience already knows.

## Done when

- [ ] The presentation was given, timed, to someone seeing the capstone for the first time.
- [ ] Their questions are recorded honestly.
- [ ] The outline was revised to answer at least one of those questions directly.
