# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Write your case study in your own voice.

## Task

1. Rewrite the opening paragraph of `case-study.md` so it clearly reflects your own voice, language background, or community — not a generic, templated tone.
2. Read both versions aloud (the generic one and your revised one) and note which one sounds more like something you would actually say.
3. Keep the version that sounds like you, even if it is less "formal."

## Why this matters

A case study is a personal record of your own work, not a corporate press release. The strongest capstone case studies XR Camp mentors read are the ones that sound like a real person telling their own story.

## Done when

- [ ] The opening paragraph was rewritten at least once.
- [ ] Both versions were compared honestly.
- [ ] The final version sounds like the learner who wrote it.
