# Stage 5 rubric: Testing, Launch, and Presentation

| Level | Release | Case study | Presentation | Retrospective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Not yet** | No final test pass, or repository missing README/licence | Lists only successes | Not given to a real audience | Vague ("communicate better") |
| **Meets expectations** | Full test pass run; repository public with README and licence | States the problem, what was built, what was left out, and at least one struggle | Given once, fits its planned time | Specific notes across all five stages |
| **Strong** | Live link verified in a fresh browser session | Case study clearly connects back to the Stage 1 brief and Stage 4 changelog | Includes a working demonstration or clear screenshots | Notes are actionable for a future capstone, not only descriptive |
| **Exceptional** | Presentation tested on a first-time audience with recorded questions (Challenge 1) | Written in the learner's own voice (Challenge 2) | Anticipates likely audience questions | Includes a concrete next-stage plan (Challenge 3) |

This stage, and the capstone, is approved when the release is genuinely public and working, and the case study honestly represents what was built and learned.
