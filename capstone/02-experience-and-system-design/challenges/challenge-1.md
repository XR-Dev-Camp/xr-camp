# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Test your wireframes against a real person before you build anything.

## Task

1. Show your wireframes (`wireframes.md`) and primary user journey (`journeys.md`) to a friend, classmate, or family member, without explaining anything first.
2. Ask them to describe, in their own words, what they think each screen or scene is for and what they would do first.
3. Write a short "What I learned" note at the end of `wireframes.md`, listing at least one thing that confused them and how you will change the design.

## Why this matters

A wireframe that only makes sense to its author is not yet a design. Five minutes of real feedback, before any code exists, is far cheaper than discovering the same confusion in Stage 3's prototype.

## Done when

- [ ] A real person outside your own head reviewed the wireframes and journey.
- [ ] At least one point of confusion is recorded honestly.
- [ ] The wireframes or journey were updated in response.
