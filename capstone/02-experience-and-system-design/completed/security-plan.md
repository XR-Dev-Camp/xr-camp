# Security and privacy plan

## What data is collected

None. The exhibit does not use forms, accounts, cookies, or any analytics script. No visitor data is collected, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

## Where it is stored

Not applicable — nothing is collected.

## Who can access it

Not applicable — nothing is collected.

## Known risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| A future stage adds a contact form or analytics without updating this plan | This document is reviewed again at the start of every later stage, per the Stage 1 stakeholder communication plan |
| Third-party CDN scripts (the pinned three.js build) could change behaviour on their own server | Only the exact pinned version from `versions.json` is loaded, over HTTPS, from a well-known CDN |
