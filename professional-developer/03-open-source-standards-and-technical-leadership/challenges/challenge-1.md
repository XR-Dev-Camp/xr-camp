# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

File the spec issue you planned in `standards-plan.md`, for real.

## Task

1. Re-read the issue title and body you drafted in section 4 of `standards-plan.md`.
2. Search the repository's existing issues for anything close to a duplicate. If you find one, add a comment there instead of opening a new issue, and record that in your plan.
3. If it is genuinely new, open the issue on the real repository, using GitHub's issue form.
4. Add the issue's URL to `standards-plan.md`, section 4, and to `contribution-record.md` if this is also the contribution you are recording.
5. Wait at least a day. Whatever response arrives, including none yet, record it honestly.

## Why this matters

A plan that stays a plan teaches nothing about how standards work in practice. Filing a real issue, on a real repository, with real maintainers who may or may not respond quickly, is the only way to find out whether your issue was actually clear.

## Done when

- [ ] You searched for duplicates before filing.
- [ ] A real issue exists at a real URL, and that URL is recorded in your plan.
- [ ] You checked back at least once and recorded what happened, even if the answer is "no response yet."
