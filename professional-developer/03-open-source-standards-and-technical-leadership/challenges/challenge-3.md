# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional stretch.** Roughly 90 minutes.

Write a second decision record for a harder, more contested choice, and get one real review of it.

## Task

1. Add `mini-project/decision-record-0002-<your-topic>.md`, using the same four headings as decision record 0001 (Status, Context, Decision, Consequences).
2. Pick a genuinely contested choice for `scene-description-lint` (or your renamed project from Challenge 2): for example, whether it should fail a build or only warn, how it should treat a scene with no canvas at all, or whether it should support A-Frame's older `<a-text>` syntax.
3. Write the Context section so it fairly states the strongest argument for the option you did not choose, before explaining why you chose the other one.
4. Ask a classmate, or someone from your chosen Community Group, to read it and leave one comment. Record their comment and your response, even if you did not change your decision.

## Why this matters

Most real technical decisions are contested, not obvious. A decision record that only argues for the answer you already picked is weaker than one that shows you understood the alternative and chose anyway. Real technical leadership includes writing decisions well enough that someone who disagreed with you at the time can still follow your reasoning years later.

- [ ] `decision-record-0002-*.md` exists, using the same four headings.
- [ ] The Context section states the rejected alternative's strongest argument fairly.
- [ ] A real reviewer's comment is recorded, along with your response.
