## Notes on filling in the real thing

- Use a real photo of yourself if you are comfortable with that, or leave the space blank; either is normal, and a photo is never required to apply for work.
- Do not list a skill you have not actually practised. A profile that reads honestly is more useful than one that reads impressively.
- If you decide to make an account on a real networking service, check what that service's policy is on your account being reachable from your country before relying on it for client contact; where a service is unreliable, a personal site (like your `index.html`) works everywhere.
