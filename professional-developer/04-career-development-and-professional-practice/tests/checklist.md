# Project check

Work through this list before you submit.

## The CV
- [ ] `index.html` has one clear `<h1>` with your name, and a logical heading order.
- [ ] The contact list uses only safe, professional information: no home address, no personal phone number.
- [ ] The skills table has a caption, `<th scope="col">` for column headers, and `<th scope="row">` for each skill area.
- [ ] Every skill listed is one you have actually practised in this course.
- [ ] Every project entry links to real, finished work.

## Print stylesheet
- [ ] `print.css` is linked with `media="print"` and does not affect the on-screen page.
- [ ] Printing (or print-previewing) the CV shows black text on a plain background, with link destinations printed after external links.
- [ ] The "Print or save as PDF" button and the navigation are hidden in the print output.

## The case study
- [ ] `case-study.html` names a real problem, your real role, and the real steps of your approach.
- [ ] Any performance number is stated as "on my machine," never as a general claim.
- [ ] It names at least one honest trade-off or thing you would do differently.

## LinkedIn-style profile and interview prep
- [ ] The headline and "about" section describe real work, in your own words.
- [ ] The portfolio walkthrough script is specific to your own project, not a generic list of features.
- [ ] At least five real questions to ask an interviewer are written down.

## Freelancing and contracts
- [ ] The proposal template is filled in for a real or imagined project, with a clear "what is not included" line.
- [ ] The hours estimate is a range, includes a buffer, and contains no price in any currency.
- [ ] The invoicing section lists everything a real invoice needs.
- [ ] Payment options are described in general terms only, with a note to check what is available for the specific pair of countries involved.
- [ ] `contract-overview.md` keeps the "not legal advice; laws differ by country" warning, unchanged, near the top.
- [ ] No salary figure, rate, or "typical market price" claim appears anywhere in the package.

## Accessibility
- [ ] `index.html` passes a WCAG 2.2 AA check (for example, `pa11y`).
- [ ] The keyboard can reach every link and button.
- [ ] Focus is visible at all times.
- [ ] Colour contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA.
- [ ] Every list styled with `list-style: none` has `role="list"`.

## Responsiveness
- [ ] The page works at mobile width.
- [ ] Nothing overflows horizontally.

## Quality
- [ ] The browser console has no errors on `index.html` or `case-study.html`.
- [ ] Comments explain intent, not syntax.
- [ ] No invented facts appear anywhere in the package; anything uncertain is phrased cautiously or left out.
