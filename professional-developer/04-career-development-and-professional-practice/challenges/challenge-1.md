# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Get one real person to read your CV and case study, and record their feedback honestly.

## Task

1. Send your finished `index.html` and `case-study.html` to one real person: a classmate, a mentor, a friend, or anyone who can give an honest reaction. Sharing a printed PDF works too.
2. Ask them two specific questions instead of "what do you think": "Is there anything here you don't understand?" and "Is there anything that makes you want to ask a follow-up question?"
3. Write down what they said, word for word where you can, even if it stings a little.
4. Make at least one real change to your CV or case study based on their feedback, and note what you changed and why in a short paragraph at the end of `freelancing-basics.md` or your learner journal.
5. If nobody is available immediately, read your own CV out loud instead, slowly, the way a stranger would; you will still catch real problems this way, though a second person is better when you can arrange it.

## Why this matters

A CV and a case study are written to be read by someone who does not already know you or your work. You cannot judge that from the inside; a real reader, even one reader, finds things you cannot see in your own writing.

## Done when

- [ ] A real person (or, if unavailable, a careful read-aloud of your own) reviewed your CV and case study.
- [ ] Their actual feedback is recorded, not summarised into something more flattering.
- [ ] At least one real change was made in response, and is noted.
