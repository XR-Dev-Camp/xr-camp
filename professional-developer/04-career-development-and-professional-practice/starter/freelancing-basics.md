# Freelancing basics

This document is a starting map, not a complete business course. It covers four things every new freelancer needs on day one: finding clients, writing a proposal, estimating honestly, and sending an invoice. Do not put a real rate, fee, or "typical price" figure anywhere in this document: prices vary by country and currency, and this is a personal planning document, not a market survey.
