## Where to learn more

Contract law is set at the country (and sometimes regional) level, so a single lesson cannot tell you what is enforceable where you live or where your client lives. If a project is small and low-risk, a short, clear written agreement covering the four areas above is far better than nothing. If it is not, a qualified local professional, or a free or low-cost legal-aid service where one exists in your country, is worth the time.
