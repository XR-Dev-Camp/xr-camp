# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional stretch.** Roughly 90 minutes.

Write the other deliverable too: if you wrote a lesson for Challenges 1-2, write a short mentor plan as well, or the reverse.

## Task

1. Copy whichever template you have not yet used from `starter/lesson-template/` or `starter/mentor-plan-template.md`.
2. Write a second, smaller deliverable: a mentor plan for the same topic you just taught, naming how you would guide someone through your own lesson session by session; or, if you wrote the mentor plan first, a tiny one-session lesson (a single 45-minute session, one visible outcome, one accessible completed page) on any beginner topic you know well.
3. Cross-reference the two: your mentor plan should mention how a mentor would use your lesson (or a lesson like it) as one of its worked examples, and your lesson's README should note, in one sentence, how a mentor might introduce it.
4. If your second deliverable includes a completed page with any 3D content, it must still pass the same accessibility checklist in `tests/checklist.md`, including the "3D and XR (manual)" section.

## Why this matters

Teaching and mentoring are two sides of the same skill: a lesson gives a stranger a clear, self-contained path; a mentor plan gives one specific person a guided, adaptive one. Real instructors at XR Camp will often do both. Trying the deliverable you did not choose shows you which parts of "accessible teaching," "giving feedback," and "handling harm" generalize between the two, and which do not.

## Done when

- [ ] A second, complete deliverable exists (a short lesson or a mentor plan, whichever you had not written).
- [ ] The two deliverables reference each other in at least one place.
- [ ] Any new completed page still passes `tests/checklist.md` in full, including the 3D and XR section if it applies.
