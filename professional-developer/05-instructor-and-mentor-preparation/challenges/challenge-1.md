# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Get one real person to try your lesson or mentor plan, and record their honest feedback.

## Task

1. Choose one real reader: another learner, a friend who is a beginner at whatever you taught, or a mentor.
2. If you wrote a lesson, ask them to actually work through your starter file for about 20 minutes, using only your `README.md`. Watch where they hesitate; do not explain anything out loud while they work.
3. If you wrote a mentor plan, ask them to read it as if they were about to be mentored by you, and tell you honestly whether the cadence, the feedback approach, and the safety section would make them feel supported.
4. Give them the specific, kind feedback structure from Step 7 back: ask "what worked," "what was confusing," and "what would make you trust this more."
5. Record what they said, word for word where you can, in a short new section of your `README.md` or plan called "Feedback from a real reader." Do not soften or omit anything that was actually confusing.
6. Fix at least one real problem they found before you submit.

## Why this matters

A lesson or a mentor plan that only you have ever read teaches you nothing about whether it actually works for someone else. The people XR Camp serves are beginners in a second or third language, often on a slow connection; the only way to find out whether your plain language, your pacing, and your safety section actually land is to watch a real person meet them for the first time.

## Done when

- [ ] A real person actually worked through your lesson's starter file, or read your mentor plan as if they were the mentee.
- [ ] Their real feedback is recorded honestly, including anything confusing or uncomfortable.
- [ ] At least one concrete change was made in response before you submitted.
