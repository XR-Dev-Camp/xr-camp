# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45-60 minutes.

Recolour the scene using colours that mean something in your own culture or community.

## Task

1. Choose a set of colours with real meaning to you: a flag, a textile pattern, a local festival.
2. Change each shape's `color` attribute, and the matching swatch colours in the 2D list, to your chosen palette.
3. Update `#scene-description` and the list text to name what the colours represent, in one honest sentence.
4. Re-check colour contrast: a colour chosen for meaning must still meet WCAG 2.2 AA against the scene's background and the page's text colours.

## Why this matters

The three colours in Ana's version are arbitrary. Choosing colours that mean something to you, while keeping every accessibility requirement intact, is a small, real example of what Challenge 2 in the main lesson asks: bring your own culture into the work without weakening it.

## Done when

- [ ] The palette is personally meaningful, and the lesson says why in one sentence.
- [ ] Every colour still meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast where it is used as text or against the background.
