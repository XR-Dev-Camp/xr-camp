Shapes and colour

A red box, a blue sphere, and a gold cylinder sit in a row on a grey floor. The gold cylinder turns slowly in place. Nothing else moves, and the camera never moves on its own.

The shapes, in words

  • Box, red, on the left.
  • Sphere, blue, in the middle.
  • Cylinder, gold, on the right. It turns slowly, unless animation is paused.
