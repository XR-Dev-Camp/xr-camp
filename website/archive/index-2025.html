Our Mission

XR Camp is an online school dedicated to provide women the opportunity to become web designers & developers with a focus on immersive web, 3D, and relevant metaverse technologies.

Online classes for anyone

To cover operational costs and compensate our amazing industry professional contributors, courses will be made available online for a per course fee. Since our focus is getting more women into technology, we look to use the fees collected from users to make scholarships available to any woman interested in learning these skills.

Program

These women will have the opportunity to be taught by other women experts from around the world via video workshops (Spanish & Mandarin translations available)
