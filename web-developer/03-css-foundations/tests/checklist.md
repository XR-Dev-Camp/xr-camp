# Project check

Work through this list before you submit.

## Your stylesheet
- [ ] Every page links to the same `styles.css`.
- [ ] Colours, type sizes, and spacing are custom properties on `:root`, and the rules use them.
- [ ] The stylesheet is organised in sections, each with a comment.

## Accessibility
- [ ] Every text colour pair is 4.5:1 or more, and the ratio is noted next to it.
- [ ] Focus outlines and field borders are 3:1 or more.
- [ ] Press Tab through every page: focus is always clearly visible.
- [ ] The skip link appears when you press Tab once.
- [ ] Links are underlined, and visited links look different.
- [ ] At 200% zoom, nothing is cut off and nothing needs sideways scrolling.

## Structure
- [ ] Your HTML is unchanged, apart from the `<link>`: no headings chosen for their size.
