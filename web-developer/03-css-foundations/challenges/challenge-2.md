# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

A palette from your own culture or place.

## Task

1. Choose colours from something that matters to you: a textile, a market, a festival, a landscape, a flag, a traditional painting.
2. Build a palette of five colours from it, and put them in your tokens.
3. Check every text and background pair. If a colour fails, keep it for decoration (borders, backgrounds of large shapes) and choose a darker or lighter shade for text.
4. Add a short comment in `:root` explaining where the palette comes from.

## Why this matters

A site that looks like it belongs to its community is more welcoming than one that looks like everywhere else. Checking contrast shows you how to keep a strong identity and stay readable.

## Done when

- [ ] Your palette comes from somewhere specific, and the comment says where.
- [ ] Every text pair passes 4.5:1.
