# Starter — Git, GitHub, and Publishing

Begin here.

- `index.html` is your **project collection** page: the home page of your
  repository, with ten numbered `TODO` comments. Its styles are finished.
  Copy it into the top of your project folder, next to your project folders.
- `README-template.md` is the README for your repository, with nine `TODO`
  comments. Copy it into the same place and rename it `README.md`.

Your own projects are the rest of the starter: your 3D world from Course 0.1
and your site from Courses 1.1–1.6.

Full instructions: [`../README.md`](../README.md).
