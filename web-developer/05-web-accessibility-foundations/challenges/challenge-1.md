# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Record a keyboard-only walkthrough of your repaired page.

## Task

1. Use your computer's screen recorder (on Windows, the Snipping Tool's record option or **Windows + Alt + R** with Xbox Game Bar; on a Mac, **⌘ + Shift + 5**).
2. Record yourself using the repaired page with the keyboard only: skip link, headings, the table, the link, and sending the form.
3. Then record the same with `original.html`, and stop when you get stuck.
4. Keep both recordings with your audit report.

## Why this matters

A short video of someone getting stuck is often what persuades a team to fix a problem. Showing is more powerful than a table of rule numbers.

## Done when

- [ ] You have two recordings: the repaired page working, and the original failing.
- [ ] The repaired recording uses no mouse at all.
