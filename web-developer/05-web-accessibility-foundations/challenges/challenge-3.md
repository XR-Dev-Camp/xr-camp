# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Write an accessibility statement for your site.

## Task

1. Read the W3C's guide [Developing an accessibility statement](https://www.w3.org/WAI/planning/statements/).
2. Write `accessibility.html` for your site, including:
   - your commitment, and the standard you aim for (WCAG 2.2 level AA);
   - how you tested (keyboard, screen readers, tools, devices);
   - any known problems, honestly, and when you plan to fix them;
   - how people can tell you about a problem, and how quickly you will answer.
3. Link to it from the footer of every page.

## Why this matters

No site is perfect. An honest statement tells people what to expect and how to reach you when something does not work, which builds more trust than claiming perfection.

## Done when

- [ ] Your statement covers all four points.
- [ ] Every page links to it.
