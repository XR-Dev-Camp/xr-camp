# Completed — Phase 1 Capstone: Web Developer Portfolio

The reference solution: Ana's portfolio. Open it **after** you have written
your own.

- `index.html`: Ana's home page, with four project cards and a filter.
- `case-study.html`: her case study of the Riverside Community Centre site.
- `gallery-3d.html`: her projects in a small 3D gallery, with a plain list.
- `styles.css`: the Riverside stylesheet with Ana's own colours.

Ana is fictional, so her project links point to XR Camp's reference
solutions, and she has no GitHub profile. Your links go to your own
published projects and their code.

## Comparing against your own work

Your words, colours, and projects should be different from Ana's: that is
the point. Check only:

- Does a visitor learn who you are and what you build in two sentences?
- Does your case study explain your decisions, and not only show results?
- Does everything work with the keyboard, at 320 pixels wide, and on the
  published site?
- Does the 3D gallery have a description, a keyboard route, and a list as
  its fallback?

If yes, your portfolio is ready.
