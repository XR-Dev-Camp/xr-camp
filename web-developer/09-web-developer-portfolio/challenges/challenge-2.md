# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

A personal visual identity, with a palette from your culture.

## Task

1. Choose a source for your colours that means something to you: a textile, a traditional craft, a festival, a landscape, a building in your city, or a dish your family cooks.
2. Pick four or five colours from it: one dark colour for text, one strong colour for links and buttons, one accent, one pale colour for the background band, and a background.
3. Put them in your design tokens. Check every text pair in the [WebAIM Contrast Checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/): at least 4.5:1 for text, at least 3:1 for the focus outline and borders. If a colour you love fails, use a darker version of it for text, and keep the original for large areas with no text on them.
4. Write the ratios in the comment above your tokens.
5. In your "About me" or "How I built this site" section, add one or two sentences about where your colours come from.
6. Use the same colours in your 3D gallery: the panels' `data-colour` values and the frame colours in the script.

## Why this matters

A portfolio that looks like everyone else's is easy to forget. Colours with a story make it yours, and checking them shows that you can be creative and accessible at the same time: exactly what a design team needs.

## Done when

- [ ] Every colour is a token, and every text pair is at least 4.5:1.
- [ ] The comment above your tokens lists the ratios.
- [ ] Your site says, in a sentence or two, where the colours come from.
- [ ] The 3D gallery uses the same palette.
