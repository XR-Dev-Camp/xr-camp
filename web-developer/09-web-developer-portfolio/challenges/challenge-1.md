# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

A second case study.

## Task

1. Choose a second project, different from your first case study: for example your accessibility audit from Course 1.5, or your explorer from Course 1.6.
2. Copy `case-study.html` to a new file with a clear, lower-case name, such as `case-study-explorer.html`. Change its `<title>`, its description, and its `h1`.
3. Write every section again for this project: key facts, the problem, who it is for, your process, at least three decisions, accessibility, and what you learned.
4. Link to it from the project's card on your home page, with link text that names the project: "Read the programme explorer case study".
5. Decide how the navigation should work now that there are two case studies. One simple answer: change the "Case study" link to "Case studies", pointing to your projects section.

## Why this matters

One case study can look like luck. Two show that you have a way of working: you plan, you decide for reasons, you test, and you learn. Writing the second one is also much faster, because you already know the shape.

## Done when

- [ ] The second case study has every section, and at least three decisions with a "why".
- [ ] Its card on the home page links to it with clear link text.
- [ ] The navigation is the same on every page, and still makes sense.
