# Starter — Phase 1 Capstone: Web Developer Portfolio

Begin here.

- `index.html` is your home page, with TODOs 1–4: your name, your About
  section, your project cards, and your contact details.
- `case-study.html` is a case study template, with TODOs 5–9.
- `styles.css` is the Riverside stylesheet from Courses 1.3 and 1.4, ready
  to reuse. TODO 10 gives it your colours; TODO 11 adds one wide-screen
  layout.
- `gallery-3d.html` is this lesson's 3D moment, nearly finished. TODO 12:
  add your own projects to its list, and describe the scene.
- `portfolio.js` is the project filter. It is finished: read it in Step 10.
- `centre.svg`, `centre-480.jpg`, `centre-960.jpg`, and the three
  `thumb-*.svg` files are simple drawings you may use until you have your
  own screenshots.

Full instructions: [`../README.md`](../README.md).
