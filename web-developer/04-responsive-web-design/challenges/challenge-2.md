# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

A device test log from three real devices.

## Task

1. Borrow three different devices: your phone, a friend's phone, a tablet, a library computer, an old phone in a drawer.
2. On each, open your site and check every page: can you read it, tap everything, fill in the form, and scroll the table?
3. Also try the device's biggest text size, and landscape.
4. Add a `test-log.html` page to your site: a table of device, browser, what worked, what did not, and what you fixed.

## Why this matters

Your learners, clients, and users will not have your device. Many will have older, cheaper, or smaller ones. A test log is how professional teams prove a site works, and it looks good in a portfolio.

## Done when

- [ ] Your test log covers three devices, with the browser for each.
- [ ] You fixed at least one problem the log found.
