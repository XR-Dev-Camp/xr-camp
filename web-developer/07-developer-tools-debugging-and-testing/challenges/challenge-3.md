# Challenge 3: Explorer

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Profile a real website with the Performance panel.

## Task

1. Open a large real website: a news site or an online shop.
2. Open the **Performance** panel, press the record button, reload the page, and stop recording after a few seconds.
3. Find: how long until the first content appeared, the longest single task, and which files were the largest (in the **Network** panel).
4. Do the same with your explorer. Write both results in your journal, side by side.

## Why this matters

Many learners in XR Camp use phones and connections slower than the developers of most websites. Being able to measure what makes a page slow is the first step to building pages that are fast for them.

## Done when

- [ ] Your journal compares a real site with your explorer.
- [ ] You named the three largest files on the real site.
