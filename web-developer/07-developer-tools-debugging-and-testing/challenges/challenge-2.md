# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 30 minutes.

Explain one bug, and how you found it, in your own language.

## Task

1. Choose the bug that taught you the most.
2. Write a short explanation, in the language you think in, for someone who is just starting: what the symptom was, which tool you opened, what you saw, and what the fix was.
3. Add one screenshot of the tool showing the problem.
4. Add it to your journal, or as a short post to share when the XR Camp community opens.

## Why this matters

Explaining a bug in plain words is how you check you really understood it. Explanations in Spanish, Portuguese, and Chinese are also rare and valuable: most debugging help online is in English.

## Done when

- [ ] Your explanation is in your language, with a screenshot.
- [ ] Someone new to coding could follow it.
