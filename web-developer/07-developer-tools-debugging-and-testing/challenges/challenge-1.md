# Challenge 1: Foundation

**Required.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Plant five bugs of your own, and swap with a partner.

## Task

1. Make a copy of your fixed explorer.
2. Plant five bugs: at least one in HTML, one in CSS, one in JavaScript, and one accessibility problem. Keep a secret list of what you changed.
3. Write the **symptoms** only, as a user would describe them.
4. Swap with another learner (or a friend who is learning too). Find and fix each other's bugs, and write a bug report for each.
5. Compare: did your partner find the causes you planted?

## Why this matters

Planting bugs makes you think about how things break, which makes you much faster at fixing them. And reading someone else's bug report shows you quickly whether yours are clear.

## Done when

- [ ] You planted five bugs, with symptoms written down.
- [ ] You found and reported your partner's bugs.
