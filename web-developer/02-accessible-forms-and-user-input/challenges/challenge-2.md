# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45 minutes.

Translate your form, including its hints and privacy note.

## Task

1. Make a copy of `join.html` in your own language, for example `unirse.html` or `join-zh.html`.
2. Translate every label, legend, hint, option, button, and the privacy note. Set `lang` at the top of the file.
3. Leave the `id`, `name`, `type`, and `autocomplete` values in English: they are code, not text.
4. Notice what gets longer. Spanish text is often longer than English; Chinese is often shorter.

## Why this matters

A form in someone's own language is far easier to complete correctly. Knowing which parts are text and which are code is a skill you will use in Course 6.2 when you build fully multilingual sites.

## Done when

- [ ] Every visible word is in your language, and `lang` matches.
- [ ] Every `id`, `name`, `type`, and `autocomplete` value is unchanged.
