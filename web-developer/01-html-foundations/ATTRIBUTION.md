## If you add an asset

Record it in the table above **before** you commit it: source, author, license,
and any conditions. Assets whose provenance cannot be established are not
admitted, however freely available they appear.

This applies to AI-generated assets too — record the tool, the prompt, and the
terms under which the output may be used.

You will ask learners to do this in their own projects. The repository has to
model it.
