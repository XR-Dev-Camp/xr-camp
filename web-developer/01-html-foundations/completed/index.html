About us

Riverside Community Centre has served the neighbourhood since 1974. We run free and low-cost activities for all ages, and our building is open to community groups who need a place to meet.

Everyone is welcome here, regardless of background, income, or ability. If a barrier stops you taking part, please get in touch and we will do what we can.
