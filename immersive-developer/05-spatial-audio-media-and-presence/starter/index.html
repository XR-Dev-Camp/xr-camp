The exhibit gets a voice

The same exhibit from WebXR Foundations (4.1): a clay pot, a woven basket ring, and a jade stone. Each pedestal can now play its own quiet sound, spatial audio that gets louder as you move closer to it. A small screen above the exhibit shows a short audio guide with captions, and the same words are always written out below it, too.

    Transcript

    The full text of the audio guide, always here, whether or not you have played it (WCAG 1.2.1).

      1. Look around

      Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the exhibit (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

      2. Animation

      The jade stone turns slowly, and the story screen keeps changing colour, on their own. Nothing else moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it, on a screen or in a headset.

      3. Enter VR

      If your browser and a VR headset support WebXR, this button appears and lets you view the exhibit at full size. It works seated: nothing asks you to stand, reach high, or turn all the way around.

      4. Pedestal sounds

      Each of the three pedestals plays its own quiet, looping sound. Nothing plays until you press this button.
