# Design rationale — Spatial UX Design

Every spatial redesign needs a written reason behind it, not just working code. Fill this in as you build, in your own words. Short answers are fine; specific ones are better than general ones.

## Scale and distance

- Which waypoint did you set as the *default* starting distance, and why?
- What did the angular-size readout show you at "Very close" and "Far"? Did either one surprise you?

## Locking strategy

- Why does the progress kiosk stay world-locked always, instead of letting the learner switch it too?
- Of world-, body-, and view-locked, which felt best for the goals panel while you were testing it, and why?
- What is one kind of content you would *never* make view-locked, and one you would?

## Locomotion

- Did you test smooth movement as well as teleporting? Describe the difference in how each one felt.
- What happened with reduced motion turned on?

## Personal space

- What is the personal-space ring for? What would go wrong if a panel were placed inside it?

## Sources consulted

List anything you read to make these decisions (a platform's design guidelines, a WCAG success criterion, a classmate's experience). Note whether it is your own testing or somebody else's guidance — this repo treats reading-distance and text-size numbers as a starting point to test, not a fixed rule, and your rationale should say the same.
