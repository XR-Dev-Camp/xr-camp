# Challenge 2: Creative

**Optional.** Roughly 45–60 minutes.

Make the exhibit yours: replace one pedestal, its sound, and its caption with something from your own culture, community, or language.

## Task

1. Pick one exhibit (clay pot, basket ring, or jade stone) to replace with an object meaningful to you — a craft, an instrument, a food, or a building.
2. In `js/exhibit.js`, change that entry's `name`, `material`, and `caption` to describe your object honestly, in your own words.
3. Change its `geometry` and `color` in `js/app.js` if the built-in shapes (cylinder, torus, icosahedron) do not suit it; a simple box or sphere is fine.
4. Generate a new short sound for it (reuse 4.5's Python script if you have it, or write a short silent placeholder and say so in `ATTRIBUTION.md`), or reuse one of the existing three sounds and say which.
5. Update the transcript and `ATTRIBUTION.md` to match.

## Why this matters

An exhibit that only ever shows a clay pot, a basket, and a jade stone teaches one culture's objects by default. Choosing what belongs in a shared space is a real design decision, not a cosmetic one.

## Done when

- [ ] One exhibit now describes something from your own culture, community, or language.
- [ ] The caption, the transcript, and `ATTRIBUTION.md` all describe it consistently.
- [ ] The rest of the capstone (Enter VR, grab, Personalize, boundary) still works unchanged.
