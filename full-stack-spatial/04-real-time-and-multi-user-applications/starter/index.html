Meet in a small shared room

Course 5.3 gave every learner's account somewhere to save its own work. This lesson connects several signed-in accounts to each other, at the same time: sign in, and everyone else with this page open right now shares one small room. Move around with the buttons below, say something in the chat, and watch the room update for everyone else within a tenth of a second — without ever refreshing the page.

          Before you reuse this

          This lesson's server keeps chat messages and positions only in memory — nothing about what anyone says or where they stand is ever written to a database. The one thing that is saved is a report (see the reports table): a reporter's own account, the account they reported, a reason, and an optional short excerpt. Never build a real product's moderation tooling from a course project without a safety and legal review.
