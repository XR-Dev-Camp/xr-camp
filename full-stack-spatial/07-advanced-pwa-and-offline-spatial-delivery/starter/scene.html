    Look around

    Drag the view with a mouse or a finger. Or focus the scene (press Tab) and use the arrow keys. These two buttons do the same as dragging left or right, without a mouse.

    Animation

    The jade stone turns on its own, and each model plays its own built-in animation once it loads. Nothing else moves by itself, and the camera never moves unless you move it.
