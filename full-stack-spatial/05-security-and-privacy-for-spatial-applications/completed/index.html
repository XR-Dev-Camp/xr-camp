Review and fix: a small spatial app

This is the fixed version of Course 5.4's small spatial app -- accounts, saved scenes with an optional rough location, annotations left on a scene, and a shared chat room. Every one of the starter's seven vulnerabilities is fixed here: sanitised, escaped, or checked, and explained in this folder's README. It still runs only on your own computer (127.0.0.1).

                      Before you reuse this

                      Even fixed, this remains a course project, not an audited production service: the SQLite file lives on disk with no encryption at rest, there is no backup or migration-rollback story beyond Course 5.3's, and no one but you has reviewed it.
